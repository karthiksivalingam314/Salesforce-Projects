## Requirement

Retrieve the Omni-Channel configuration from the TVM working org: the service channels, the queues connected to them, and all other related Omni-Channel configuration. Document it in the respective folders and files, then push it to GitHub.

NOT PROVIDED

## Scope

- Read-only queries and metadata retrieval from `Fullsandox`.
- Documentation under `07 - Technical Documents/Omni-Channel/`, plus a summary in `02 - Knowledge Base/Architecture.md`.
